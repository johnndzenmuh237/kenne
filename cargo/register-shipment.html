How Notifications Work

  1. Submit this form when a shipment is received and ready to move.
  2. A unique KC-YYYY-XXXXXX tracking number is generated instantly.
  3. It's logged to the notification bell (top right) for your team.
  4. A ready-to-send email/WhatsApp message is prepared for the client with their tracking link.
  5. The client can track live progress any time at Kenne Cargo Tracking.
