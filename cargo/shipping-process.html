Shipping Process

From your first quote to collecting your goods in Douala.

Home› Shipping Process
01

Request a Quote

02

Receive Your Quote

KENNE calculates the applicable shipping cost instantly, based on our current rates.

03

Send Goods to Our China Warehouse

Your goods are received and registered under your tracking number.

04

Goods are prepared, consolidated, and readied for shipment.

05

Goods leave the warehouse and move to the airport or seaport.

06

Goods are transported toward Cameroon by air or sea.

07

Arrival in Douala

You receive an arrival notification with collection instructions.

08

Collection

You follow the collection instructions and pick up your goods.

09

Completed

Your shipment is marked completed in our system.
