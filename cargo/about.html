Our Story

Built on Trust, Route by Route

Kenne Cargo was founded in Douala by a team that saw how hard it was for everyday buyers and businesses in Cameroon to source, buy, and ship goods from China safely and affordably. We set out to make it simple.

What started with a handful of sourcing and shipping requests has grown into a full-service cargo partner — buying cars, sourcing suppliers, shipping by sea and air, clearing customs, delivering door to door, and handling currency exchange, all along one route: China to Cameroon.

Our mission has never changed: deliver your goods safely, on time, and at a fair price — with a tracking number and real support, so you can let us handle it and relax.
