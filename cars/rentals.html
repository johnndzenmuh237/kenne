Rental Terms & Conditions

Clear rules so there are no surprises. By booking a rental, you agree to the conditions below.

👤 Who Can Rent

  • Renter must be at least 23 years old for self-drive rentals.
  • A valid driver's license (held for 2+ years) is required for self-drive; international visitors may need an international driving permit.
  • A valid national ID or passport must be presented and a copy kept on file for the duration of the rental.

💰 Deposit & Payment

  • A refundable security deposit is required before pick-up (amount depends on vehicle class — ask on WhatsApp).
  • Payment can be made now or on delivery/pick-up — cash, mobile money, or bank transfer.
  • The deposit is refunded within 48 hours of the car being returned in its original condition, minus any charges below.

⛽ Fuel & Mileage

  • Vehicles are handed over with a full tank and must be returned full — refuelling charges apply otherwise.
  • Rentals include a standard 200 km/day mileage allowance; extra kilometers are billed at a per-km rate.
  • Rentals with a driver include the driver's fuel and standard working hours (8am–8pm); night/overtime hours are billed separately.

🛡️ Insurance & Damage

  • All rental vehicles carry third-party insurance; comprehensive cover can be added on request.
  • The renter is responsible for traffic fines, tolls, and damage caused during the rental period, up to the deposit amount.
  • Any accident or mechanical issue must be reported to Kenne Cars immediately by phone or WhatsApp.

⏰ Pick-up, Return & Late Fees

  • The rental day runs on a 24-hour cycle from the agreed pick-up time.
  • Late returns are charged a 10% per hour late fee after a 30-minute grace period; a full extra day is charged after 4 hours.
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pick-up; cancellations after that forfeit 20% of the deposit.

🚫 Not Permitted

  • Sub-renting the vehicle to a third party.
  • Driving outside Cameroon without prior written approval.
  • Off-road use with vehicles not rated for it, or overloading beyond the stated seat count.

Questions about any of these terms? Chat with our team before you book — we're happy to walk you through it.
