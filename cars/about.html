Our Story

Part of the Kenne Group

Kenne Car Business grew out of Kenne Group's cargo and sourcing operations between China and Cameroon. Having already helped thousands of clients import cars, we built a dedicated showroom and rental fleet so buyers can browse, compare, and buy with confidence — without navigating the import process themselves.

Today we sell wholesale and retail vehicles, rent cars for every occasion, and stock genuine parts — all backed by the same "let us handle it, you relax" promise as the rest of the Kenne Group.
