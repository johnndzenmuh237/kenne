What We Use

This website uses your browser’s local storage rather than traditional tracking cookies for core functionality. This keeps your shopping cart, saved shipping quotes, shipment records and display preferences on your own device.

Strictly Necessary

Cart contents, saved quotes, tracking records and your dark-mode preference. These make the site work and cannot be switched off without breaking functionality. They stay on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Analytics (Optional)

If enabled by the site owner, we may use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful and where visitors have trouble. Analytics only loads after you accept it in the cookie banner. You can decline and the site will work normally. We do not send personal details such as your name or email to analytics.

Managing Cookies

You can clear local storage and cookies at any time in your browser settings. You can also change your choice using the cookie banner, which reappears if you clear your stored preference.

Third Parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and some images from Unsplash; these providers may see your IP address as part of serving the file. WhatsApp links open WhatsApp’s own service, governed by their privacy policy.
