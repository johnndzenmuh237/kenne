KENNE operates across three major sectors: technology and electronics, China sourcing and importation, and automotive services.

Yes. Kenne Cargo provides China sourcing and importation assistance for customers looking for products and suppliers.

Can I buy products in wholesale quantities?+

Yes. KENNE provides wholesale solutions across selected technology, electronics, automotive, and other product categories.
