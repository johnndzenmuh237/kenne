Overview

This policy explains when a refund may be available. Because KENNE operates three different divisions, terms vary by what you purchased.

Electronics and Accessories

If a device is faulty on arrival, contact us within [X] days with your order number and photos. Verified faulty items may be repaired, replaced or refunded. Items damaged by misuse, liquid or unauthorised repair are not covered. Sealed software or consumable accessories may be non-returnable once opened.

Vehicles

Vehicles are sold as inspected. Once a vehicle is collected and payment completed, sales are generally final unless otherwise agreed in writing. Any warranty offered will be stated on your invoice.

Car Parts

Unused parts in original packaging may be returned within [X] days. We cannot accept returns on fitted or modified parts. Check compatibility before ordering — we list compatibility as a guide, not a guarantee.

Cargo and Shipping Fees

Shipping fees cover work already performed and are generally non-refundable once goods have departed our warehouse. If we cancel a shipment before dispatch, we will refund the shipping fee.

How to Request a Refund
