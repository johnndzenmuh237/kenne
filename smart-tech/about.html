Our Story

Part of the Kenne Group

Kenne Smart Technology grew out of Kenne Group's sourcing and cargo operations between China and Cameroon. Having already helped clients import electronics for years, we built a dedicated storefront so anyone can browse, compare, and buy genuine devices without the hassle of importing themselves.

Every phone we sell — brand new or UK used — is tested and verified before it reaches you, backed by the same "let us handle it, you relax" promise as the rest of the Kenne Group.
